Slide 6

The Next Step: Autodesk Cloud Automation

Design Automation for Revit (DA4R)

Run Revit tasks in the cloud without opening Revit, so jobs can run at scale or on a schedule.

Data Exchange

Share selected model data between tools and teams without exchanging whole files.

Other APS modules

Cloud APIs to connect models on Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) with dashboards and apps.

Our plugin logic is built to move to the cloud, starting with AutoSlopeByPoint.

Slide 17

Power BI: Live Dashboards for Construction

What It Does

Turns project data into dashboards and reports that refresh from live sources.

Data Sources at IKK

Excel, SharePoint lists, plugin exports and Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC) data through the Autodesk API.

Construction Uses

Progress and S-curve, cost and BOQ, material tracking, clashes, issues and model health.

Sharing and Licensing

Reports are shared on web and mobile by role. Licenses are to be confirmed.

The dashboard mockups are on slides 5 to 12.

Slide 18

Power Apps: Instant Reporting from Site

What It Does

Forms and small apps that site teams fill in on a phone, with photos and location.

Apps for the Site

Daily progress report, material delivery log, inspection checklist and issue report.

Where the Data Goes

Into a SharePoint list, then straight into the Power BI dashboards.

What It Needs

Built and maintained by the BIM team. Licenses are to be confirmed.

The site-to-dashboard flow is on slide 13.

Slide 19

Power Automate: Automating Construction Workflows

Approvals

RFIs, submittals and change orders go to the right person, with reminders when overdue.

Notifications

Alert the team when a clash, issue or delivery status changes.

Data Sync

Move ACC and Power Apps data into SharePoint and Power BI on a schedule.

Replaces email chains and manual follow-up.

Power Pages: Portals for Subcontractors and Clients

Subcontractors

Clients and Consultants

View approved dashboards and status.

Secure Access

One controlled entry point instead of email and shared folders.

Copilot Studio: Chat Assistant for Site Teams

Slide 22

Dataverse and AI Builder: Central Data and Built-in AI

Dataverse

One secure data store for all Power Platform apps, with roles and an audit history.

AI Builder

Reads text from invoices, delivery notes and forms and fills in the fields automatically.

Licensing

May need extra licenses beyond standard Microsoft 365, to be confirmed.

Gives Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI one shared data foundation.

Slide 23

Rollout Plan for IKK

Phase 1: Pilot

Run selected plugins on one project, collect feedback and measure the time saved.

Timeline: Month 1

Phase 2: Standardize

Shared installer, version control, training and a support process for project teams.

Timeline: Months 2 to 3

Phase 3: Scale

Extend across IKK projects and move heavy tasks to the cloud with DA4R.

Timeline: Months 4 to 9
